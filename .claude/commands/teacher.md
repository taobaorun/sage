description: Interactive mastery-based teaching — deeply understand code, PRs, bug fixes, or technical topics
---

Invoke the teacher skill.

Begin by understanding what the user wants to learn. Ask clarifying questions about:
1. The topic or code change they want to understand
2. Their current familiarity level
3. What depth they want to reach

Then guide them through interactive, mastery-based learning — quizzing, explaining, and verifying understanding at each step.
